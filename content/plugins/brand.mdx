## What it does

brand is where a project's identity starts. It interviews you (name, audience,
personality, the promise, the character), writes a `BRAND.md`, and draws you a
mascot: a single clean monoline SVG derived from the personality. `BRAND.md` is
the root of the context stack, so `VOICE.md` and `DESIGN.md` read from it.

## What you can do with it

- "Set up the brand for this project."
- "Give us a mascot."
- "Define our personality and positioning."
- "Seed a voice and a design direction from the brand."

## How it works

`/brand` asks only what it can't infer, writes `BRAND.md`, then generates a monoline
SVG character (`currentColor`, one stroke weight, reads at 24px) with two or three
traits pulled from the personality. It offers to seed starting points for `VOICE.md`
and `DESIGN.md`, pointing to the `voice` and `design` plugins to go deeper. It
augments existing files; it never clobbers them.

## Install

- Add the marketplace: `/plugin marketplace add sherizan/designagent`
- Install: `/plugin install brand@designagent`
- Run: `/brand` to define the brand and draw the mascot.
